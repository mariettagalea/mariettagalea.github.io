Marietta Galea

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Malta, supervised by Adrian Francalanza and Giovanni Bernardi. My research focuses on runtime monitoring, concurrent systems and formal methods. I also form part of the VELOCiTY research group.

Prior to starting my PhD, I obtained a BSc. (Hons.) in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Malta. I am interested in developing mathematically rigorous techniques for proving the correctness of runtime monitors.

Research Interests

  • Runtime monitoring
  • Porgramming language semantics
  • Formal methods
  • Concurrent systems
